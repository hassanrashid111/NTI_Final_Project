Operations Research & Inventory Optimization

Calculated Safety Stock (SS), Reorder Point (ROP), and Recommended Order Quantity (ROQ).

How it works: For each store-SKU pair, the system calculates Safety Stock (buffer against demand variability), Reorder Point (when to order), Target Stock Level (maximum desired stock), and Recommended Order Quantity (how many units to procure).

Safety Stock

SS = Z · σ_d · √L

Reorder Point

ROP = (d·L) + SS

Target Stock Level

TSL = ROP + (d·R)

Order Quantity

ROQ = max(0, TSL−S)
