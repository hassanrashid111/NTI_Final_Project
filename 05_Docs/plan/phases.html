Advanced Models (CatBoost · LightGBM · TFT)
4 أيام ○ Pending ▼

🌲 CatBoost

1.5 يوم

Categorical features بدون encoding. مقاوم للـ Overfitting. يدعم GPU.

HYPERPARAMETERS
  • iterations: 1000-5000
  • learning_rate: 0.01-0.1
  • depth: 6-10
  • l2_leaf_reg: 3-10

⚡ LightGBM

1.5 يوم

الأسرع في Gradient Boosting. Leaf-wise growth. Low memory.

HYPERPARAMETERS
  • n_estimators: 1000-5000
  • learning_rate: 0.01-0.1
  • num_leaves: 31-255
  • min_child_samples: 20-100

🧠 TFT

1 يوم

Temporal Fusion Transformer. مصمم للـ Time Series. Multi-horizon.

ARCHITECTURE
  • LSTM Encoder + Decoder
  • Multi-head Attention
  • Variable Selection Network
  • Quantile Regression
