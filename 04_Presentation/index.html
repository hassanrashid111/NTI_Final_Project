Mathematical Equations

Safety Stock (SS):

SS = Z_{0.95} · σ_d · √L

Reorder Point (ROP):

ROP = (d_avg · L) + SS

Recommended Order Qty (ROQ):

ROQ = max(0, TSL − Current Stock)

Inventory Risk Queue & Automated Purchase Orders

Prioritizing SKUs below Reorder Point (ROP) for Procurement Action

🔴 CRITICAL_UNDERSTOCK

Current Stock < ROP

High stockout probability. Triggers an automated emergency Purchase Order (PO) with quantity $ROQ = TSL - \text{Stock}$.

🟢 OPTIMAL_STOCK

ROP ≤ Stock ≤ TSL

Inventory buffer is operating within healthy boundaries. No immediate procurement action required.

🟡 OVERSTOCK

Current Stock > TSL

Excessive inventory level. Pauses procurement to prevent holding cost inflation and warehouse congestion.
